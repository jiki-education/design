"Ready to get started?" - mid-page CTA ideas

Thirteen replacements for the dark gradient bar between the parallel-tracks section and the two track cards (ideas 9 and 10 are built for a different slot: the gap between the testimonials wall and the Exercism band). The page around it has become a paper wall (torn tape, prints, sticky notes, hand-drawn arrows, dot grid), and the bar is now the only thing in that stretch that reads as an advert dropped in rather than part of the page. Every idea below is built from things the page already does.
