Our latest news - section ideas

Six treatments for the blog band at the very bottom of the landing page. Today it is six cards with clamped excerpts - the deepest section on the page, sitting after the reader has already been asked to sign up twice. Every idea below uses the same six real posts.

1. Featured lead + headline list

One story gets the image and a real excerpt; the rest are title-and-date rows beside it. Roughly half the depth of the current grid, and the reader is given one thing to read rather than six to choose between.
