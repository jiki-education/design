Above the Fold

Landing page hero concepts — outcome-led headline, social proof, live code preview, persona tabs, stripped-back minimal, four exploring i18n (language-list callout, rotating "Learn in Spanish" headline, i18n as a headline feature, and an auto-localized single-language hero), plus swatches of checkmark and CTA button style variants.

What Do Our Students Think?

Eight heading and intro copy options for the section (current baseline, "Don't take our word for it", a doubt-led "Not sure you're the coding type?", a number-led opener, a short quiet version, an emotional "The moment it clicks", a playful one and a direct-address one), each with a note on which layouts it suits — plus twelve concepts for the testimonials section — infinite scrolling marquee rows, scattered polaroid-style cards, a confetti spotlight carousel, a bold sticker wall, three with profile photos and student locations (photo marquee, world map pins, floating bubble cluster), a hover-flip card revealing flag, location, and skill level, refined variations on the actual landing-page quote card (baseline, accent-edged, and dark), two fun star-free takes (a pinned sticky-note wall and a group-chat bubble stream), and a 3-wide sticky-note wall using the real testimonials from the landing page.

Testimonial Heading Layouts

Eight layout treatments of one fixed piece of copy ("Thousands of beginners later… We've taught people to code in 40+ countries") so the arrangement is the only variable: centred baseline, oversized gradient first word, editorial left/right split, heading plus a three-figure stat rail, hand-drawn highlighter sweep, boxed odometer digits, two-line light/heavy weight contrast, and a left-aligned dark band. Each sits above greyed-out stand-ins for the testimonial cards that follow.

From the Team Behind Exercism

Six concepts for the Exercism credibility section — a refined version of the current centered stats layout, a dark split panel with logo badge, a bordered stat bar with pill badge, a testimonial-quote hybrid, a minimal wordmark strip, and icon stat cards on a gradient panel.

Learn to Code: feature block layouts

Four layouts for the same five callouts (errors in plain English, rewind your own code, help not answers, see what your code did, stretched never stranded) so the layout is the only variable: a big lead with four compact thumbnail rows, three cards with the last two on a tinted strip, a hub with one large product image and the callouts around it, and a 2 by 2 of four equal blocks with images large enough to read. All images, no video. Every visual is a stand-in pulled from the existing demo recording.

Project Cards

A landscape, banner-led card for Projects — the longer, multi-step build sitting alongside Challenges and Exercises. Full width of the content column, stacked one per row, with a meta row of steps, time and difficulty, tag pills and a progress footer. Three directions, each in four states (not started, in progress, complete, coming soon): a banner-led card, a version built from the journey-path row on Coding Fundamentals, and the Challenges grid tile stretched into a full-width landscape row.

Two parts: Learn to Code & Learn to Build

Seven concepts for the overview band just below the fold that explains Jiki is two things, setting up the two full-width detail cards beneath it - two colour-coded panels with ticks, a diagram of the tracks running as parallel rails week by week, interlocking puzzle pieces with copy either side, a single large sentence with "code" and "build" picked out in their colours, two taped notes on the wall, a full-bleed diptych torn into a purple half and a blue half, and a show-don't-tell pair putting a code snippet beside a frontend-API-database sketch. Every version has to land that the two run alongside each other rather than one after the other.

Ready to get started? - mid-page CTA

Eight replacements for the dark gradient bar above the two track cards, all built from things the landing page already does now that it has become a paper wall - a sticky note taped to the wall, the warm cream paper of the project prints band, a recap of three ticked outcomes beside the button, a project polaroid beside the ask, a student's question from the live Q&A answered by the CTA, the two tracks as interlocking puzzle pieces, the sign-up as one bare sentence with no block at all, and the polaroid version with the ticked outcomes underneath.

Sound fun? Join us for free — CTA

Six treatments for the sign-up CTA that sits directly under the "Games. Animations. Actual things." polaroid-prints band, so the two read as one moment instead of two unrelated sections — staying on the same cream paper with an extra taped-in print, a torn-paper edge into the existing dark band, the CTA dressed as the blank next polaroid in the row, a contact-sheet strip of film frames carrying the projects into the dark, a taped note card floating over the join, and a version where the handwritten footnote runs straight on into the ask.

Hi there, I'm Jeremy

Five concepts for the founder intro section, aimed at warmth and credibility — a portrait beside the copy with credential chips and the testimonial beneath, a polaroid-and-sticky-note version reusing the landing page's handmade language, a centred "letter from Jeremy" with two quotes underneath, a strip showing him actually teaching (headshot plus live Q&A stills), and a mash-up of the first and last — Idea 1's layout with a polaroid photo and the quiet full-width quote line. Every concept carries a testimonial that credits his teaching by name.

Get a Verified Certificate

Six concepts for the outcomes section — each making the three payoffs explicit (a verified certificate, a diverse portfolio of real projects, and a true understanding of how code works): outcome cards above a certificate hero, a dark numbered "what you'll walk away with" list, a stacked certificate with ticked outcome rows, a portfolio-first layout showing the actual projects, a compact three-column strip, and a celebratory centred layout with skill pills.

CTA Buttons & Hover Effects

Six button treatments, each with its hover state, shown on all five backgrounds the landing page actually has (white, dotted white, cream paper, hero navy, near-black) and checked against the header pair underneath — the UI Kit's own bloom-ring, a lift-and-glow, an arrow that slides while the button holds still, a hard-shadow sticker press that matches the paper-wall language, a purple-to-blue gradient sweep tying the Code and Build colours together, and an outline-to-fill secondary with a light variant for dark bands. Opens with an audit of the four different CTA buttons currently shipping in two brand colours and three corner radii, and closes with the recommended system assembled across page, hero and header.

Hi there, I'm Jeremy — without the polaroid

Eight treatments for the founder intro that give up the polaroid print, which by this point appears three times on the landing page and is weakest here — it is the only one of the three where the frame competes with the subject. Every idea carries the section's full copy, so the layouts are judged against the real volume of text. Four keep the portrait as the subject and change how it is framed: a flat solid ring, a segmented ring whose three arcs are the three credentials (what the landing page currently ships), a staff badge carrying his name and role, and an offset block of brand colour behind the photo. Four change the section's colour or layout instead: the whole band inverted to dark, a type-led version with the portrait shrunk to a mark beside the signature, a gradient colour field the photo is multiplied into, and a centred top-down stack with the copy in two columns.

Hi there, I'm Jeremy — credential pills

Seven treatments for the three credential pills in the founder intro ("2M+ people taught", "20 years building software", "Founder of Exercism") — the tightened baseline, a stat rail that finally lets "2M+" be the biggest thing in the block, pills tinted by what kind of credential they are, taped cards in the page's handmade language, a hand-drawn marker underline reusing the "master teacher" highlight, the three folded into one inline sentence, and stacked icon-led rows for narrow columns. Same three facts throughout, each shown under a stand-in of the section heading on the background it actually sits on.

Our Latest News

Six treatments for the blog band at the very bottom of the landing page, which today is six cards of clamped excerpts — the deepest section on the page, sitting after the reader has already been asked to sign up twice. A featured lead story with headline rows beside it (plus a variant where those rows carry thumbnails), the existing grid trimmed to three cards, the posts as newspaper clippings taped to the cream wall, a horizontally scrolling contact-sheet strip keeping all six, a version filed by the question the reader is asking ("Still deciding?" / "Behind the scenes") rather than by publish date, and a news-plus-newsletter split that adds a lower-commitment ask. All of them use the six real posts already on the page.
