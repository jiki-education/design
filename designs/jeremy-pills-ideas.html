Hi there, I'm Jeremy — credential pill ideas

Seven treatments for the three credential pills in the founder intro on jiki-landing.html. Same three facts throughout — only the treatment changes. Each one is shown under a stand-in of the section's heading, on the background it actually sits on, so the comparison is fair.

1

Tightened baseline

What ships today, with the vertical padding and line-height sorted out. Here as a reference point to judge the others against, not as a proposal.

Hi there! 👋 I'm Jeremy, and I've helped over two million people level up their coding skills.

2M+ people taught 20 years building software Founder of Exercism

I've spent 20 years in and around this stuff. I started out in AI, back when that meant something very different…

2

Stat rail

The number does the talking: figure at 32px, small label underneath, hairline dividers between. "2M+" stops being the smallest type in the section and becomes the largest. Speaks the same way as the Exercism figures further down the page.

3

Tinted by meaning

Each pill takes its icon's colour at a light tint. The three facts are three different kinds of thing — reach, experience, standing — and this stops them reading as one undifferentiated list of tags. A one-line change from what ships now.

Hi there! 👋 I'm Jeremy, and I've helped over two million people level up their coding skills.

2M+ people taught 20 years building software Founder of Exercism

I've spent 20 years in and around this stuff. I started out in AI, back when that meant something very different…

4

Taped cards

The facts as three small cards taped to the wall at slight angles, with the numbers in Caveat. The only idea that belongs to the paper language the rest of the page is in — but also the fourth taped-paper element by this point in the scroll.

5

Marker underline

No container at all — the facts as plain text with a hand-drawn highlighter sweep under each, reusing the treatment already on "Jeremy is a master teacher" lower in the same section. Quietest option that still separates the three.

Hi there! 👋 I'm Jeremy, and I've helped over two million people level up their coding skills.

6

Inline sentence

All three folded into one line under the heading, numbers in bold. Costs no vertical room at all and is the fastest to read — but gives up the scannability that made the pills worth having in the first place.

Hi there! 👋 I'm Jeremy, and I've helped over two million people level up their coding skills.

20 years building software, 2M+ people taught, and founder of Exercism.

I've spent 20 years in and around this stuff. I started out in AI, back when that meant something very different…

7

Icon-led rows

Stacked rather than in a row, each fact with a circular icon. Suits the layout where the copy column is narrow or the pills would otherwise wrap awkwardly — and it is the only option that never breaks onto a second line badly.
