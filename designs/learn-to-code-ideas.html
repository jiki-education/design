"Learn to Code" feature blocks: seven layouts

Seven layouts for the same five callouts, so the layout is the only variable between them. Ideas 1 to 4 are images only: a big lead with four compact thumbnail rows; three cards with the last two on a tinted strip; a hub with one large product image and the callouts around it; and a 2 by 2 of four equal blocks with images large enough to read. Ideas 5 to 7 put one running demo in the middle: callouts in the margins, callouts as stickers over the frame, and a single chaptered recording with a progress bar along the bottom. Every still is a stand-in pulled from the existing demo recording, and each one names the asset that will replace it.

1. Lead and list

One callout carries a large image; the rest run beneath as compact rows with square thumbnails. Clearest hierarchy of the three.
