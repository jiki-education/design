Hi there, I'm Jeremy — ideas without the polaroid

Six treatments for the founder intro, all avoiding the polaroid print. Same photo and the same full copy in every one — both paragraphs, the signature, the learner faces and the testimonial — so the only variable is how the portrait is presented.

📌 The constraint: the polaroid already appears three times on the landing page — the five project prints, the three prints above the mid-page CTA, and Jeremy's portrait here. This is the one that gives it up, because it is the only one where the frame competes with the subject.

1

Gradient ring

The portrait circular, inside the purple-to-blue ring the testimonial avatars already use, scaled up. Reuses a component the page has, and the gradient is the two tracks — the one bit of brand language here that carries meaning. A circle also crops a face better than a 4:5 rectangle.
