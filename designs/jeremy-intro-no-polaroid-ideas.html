Hi there, I'm Jeremy — ideas without the polaroid

Six treatments for the founder intro, all avoiding the polaroid print. Same photo and the same full copy in every one — both paragraphs, the signature, the learner faces and the testimonial — so the only variable is how the portrait is presented.

2

Segmented ring

The ring split into three arcs in the credential colours — purple, blue, green — so it reads as the three facts wrapped around him rather than as decoration. The one version where the ring carries information.

Hi there! 👋 I'm Jeremy, and I've helped over millions of people level up their coding skills, all around the world.

Senior Developer Passionate teacher Mission Driven

I've spent 20 years in and around this stuff. I started out in AI, back when that meant something very different, and ended up co-inventing a patented system still used in heart surgery today. Segmented ring — colour badges, square portrait

The sticker idea with the black taken out: each badge is built from a single hue — pale fill, mid border, dark text, lighter offset shadow — so the outline belongs to the colour instead of sitting on top of it. Softer than 2c and it keeps the section inside the brand palette. The portrait is also shown differently here: a rounded square with a purple block offset behind it, so you can see the section without a circle in it.
