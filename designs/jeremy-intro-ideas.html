Meet Jeremy — section ideas

Five ways to make the "Hi there, I'm Jeremy" section feel welcoming and credible — with a photo and a testimonial that credits his teaching. Copy is identical in every concept; only the layout changes.

Idea 1 — Portrait & proof

Photo on the left in a soft rounded frame, copy on the right. Credential chips turn his claims into scannable proof, and the testimonial sits directly beneath the whole block so it reads as a reply to what he just said.
