Testimonial Heading Layouts — Ideas

Eight layout and typographic treatments of the same copy (option 4, "Leads with the number", from testimonials-ideas.html). The words stay fixed throughout — only the arrangement, emphasis and scale change — so the layout is the only variable. Each sits above a greyed-out hint of the testimonial cards that would follow.

1. Centred stack (baseline)

What the current section does — big centred heading, one line of intro. Safe, calm, gives the number no special treatment.

Thousands of beginners later…

We've taught people to code in 40+ countries. Here's what a few of them had to say.

2. Oversized first word

"Thousands" set huge in the brand gradient, with the rest of the sentence tucked under it. Makes the scale the visual, not just the claim.

3. Editorial split

Heading left, intro right, both left-aligned on a shared baseline. Feels more grown-up and reads faster on desktop.

The intro's numbers are lifted out into three figures under the heading. Strongest proof, but needs numbers we're happy to publish.
